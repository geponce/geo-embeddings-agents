## Decisions
- 2026-11-12: Project created. Start region Pinal County, Arizona (farmland and rangeland). Agents with Claude Agent SDK and Claude Opus 5.5. Databricks Free Edition.
- 2026-11-12: Databricks CLI v1.26.0 via Homebrew. Auth by OAuth browser login (U2M) into a CLI profile, token kept in the macOS keychain. The workspace has catalogs `system`, `samples` and `workspace`. Project tables will go in `workspace`.
- 2026-11-12: Package installed in editable mode (`pip install -e .`) so imports work without `PYTHONPATH`. `init_ee()` loads `.env` with python-dotenv.
- 2026-11-12: Smoke test pixel (-111.70, 32.80), 2024, returned 64 values with L2 norm 0.9991. Values repeat across bands, which suggests reduced storage precision. Fine for similarity, note it in the docs.

- 2026-11-12: Stage 2 years 2023 and 2024. 2023 is the only recent CDL year with a per-pixel confidence band, so we can test whether model errors concentrate where CDL is unsure. CDL 2025 shows Grass/Pasture dropping from 5.8% to 1.1% of Pinal, which looks like a labeling change rather than land change.
- 2026-11-12: 11 grouped classes (Shrubland, Grass/Pasture, Alfalfa, Cotton, Corn+Sorghum, Small grains, Other crops, Fallow/Idle, Developed, Barren, Woody). Water and herbaceous wetland excluded (tiny area).
- 2026-11-12: Only "pure" pixels are sampled, where the 3x3 CDL block (90 m) is one grouped class. This removes field-edge label errors from the 30 m to 10 m mismatch.
- 2026-11-12: Two-step sampling. Stratified sample on labels at the CDL 30 m grid, then read embeddings at those points. Sampling all 64 bands at 10 m over the county timed out (over 5 min) even at 50 points per class. The two-step version took 30 s for 550 points and 9 min for 44,000.
- 2026-11-12: Removed databricks-connect from the env. Its latest release for Python 3.11 pins numpy<2, which broke pyarrow and scikit-learn. Databricks code runs in the workspace instead.
- 2026-11-12: Sample QA. Labels match CDL codes 100%. Crop classes are spatially clustered (Corn+Sorghum 2000 points fall in about 300 distinct 1 km cells, Shrubland in about 1800). A random train/test split would leak. In 2023, 25% of Fallow/Idle and 21% of Small grains labels have CDL confidence under 70%.

- 2026-11-12: Spatial CV blocks of 10 km. Same-class embedding similarity falls steeply from about 0.98 within 100 m to a near-flat level by 3-5 km for Alfalfa, Cotton, Small grains and Fallow, and by about 10-12 km for Corn+Sorghum. That is field and farm scale autocorrelation, the leakage to block. Further drops at 10-100 km (Woody, Barren, Grass/Pasture, Shrubland, Other crops) reflect regional differences in vegetation and geology, which no block size removes. At 10 km every class still falls in at least 37 blocks (Corn+Sorghum is the smallest), enough for 5-fold CV. At 15 km Corn+Sorghum drops to 21 blocks. An automatic "range" rule was tried and dropped because the regional declines distort it.

- 2026-11-12: RF baseline (300 trees, sqrt features), 5-fold StratifiedGroupKFold on 10 km blocks (177 blocks). Pooled out-of-fold OA 0.913, Kappa 0.904, macro F1 0.913. Across folds OA 0.913 ± 0.019 (worst fold 0.879). Best F1 Cotton 0.97, Developed 0.97. Worst Other crops 0.81, Fallow/Idle 0.86, Grass/Pasture 0.86. Main confusions are Shrubland with Grass/Pasture (6% and 5%), Other crops spread across all crop classes, and Corn+Sorghum to Other crops (6%).
- 2026-11-12: Caveats on the baseline. (1) It measures agreement with CDL, not ground truth, and CDL has its own errors. (2) The sample is class-balanced and pure-pixel only, so this is not the accuracy of a wall-to-wall map. Shrubland covers about 80% of Pinal, and field edges and mixed pixels were excluded. (3) Clustering makes folds uneven for some classes (Corn+Sorghum ranges from 284 to 1494 test rows per fold).

- 2026-11-12: Random split vs 10 km spatial blocks, same RF and seed. OA 0.951 vs 0.913, Kappa 0.946 vs 0.904. The error rate rises from 4.9% to 8.7%, so a random split hides about 44% of the errors. Fold-to-fold spread is also understated (±0.003 vs ±0.019). The largest F1 gaps are the clustered crop classes, Other crops +0.12, Small grains +0.05, Corn+Sorghum +0.05. Clustering alone does not explain the gap across all classes (r = -0.23 with distinct 1 km cells per 1000 samples). Barren and Woody are as clustered but gap only +0.02, likely because they are easy to separate from every other class anyway. The gap is largest where classes are both clustered and hard to tell apart, so the model can lean on "where" instead of "what".

- 2026-11-12: Failure analysis of the spatial-CV RF. (1) Errors track CDL confidence (2023). Error rate 25.1% where CDL confidence is under 50, 19.6% at 50-69, 14.8% at 70-89, 9.9% at 90-98 and 3.4% at 99-100. This fits label noise in the reference, but low-confidence pixels may also be truly ambiguous places, so it does not prove the CDL label is wrong. (2) "Other crops" is not one thing. Pecans (4.8% error), misc vegetables (0.5%) and potatoes (7.5%) are learned well. Double crops are mostly predicted as Corn+Sorghum (Dbl Crop Oats/Corn 57% error, Triticale/Corn 56%), citrus and oranges as Woody or Shrubland, olives and sod as Alfalfa. Many of these "errors" are a class design problem, not a model failure. (3) The worst blocks are scattered, not one region. Fold 3 (12.1% error) is worst mainly because it holds one 663-pixel mixed farm block near Maricopa (lon -111.91, lat 33.07) with 35% error. Without that block fold 3 is at 10.2%. Other weak blocks are Grass/Pasture in the northeast mountains (about lon -111.3, lat 33.44) and Woody along the San Pedro side (lon -110.95, lat 32.78). (4) 2023 error 9.5%, 2024 7.9%.

- 2026-11-12: Class scheme v2 (13 classes). Split Double crop (CDL 26, 225-241, 254) and Orchards (tree and vine crops, mostly pecans, olives, citrus, oranges) out of Other crops. Re-pulled the sample from Earth Engine instead of relabeling, because the pure-pixel check depends on the grouping. 52,000 rows, 2000 per class per year, so no count imbalance. RF uses class_weight="balanced" anyway (no effect with equal counts). Delta table reloaded. The v1 (11-class) numbers above are kept as history and are not comparable to v2.
- 2026-11-12: Added 95% confidence intervals by block bootstrap (resample whole 10 km blocks, 1000 times) because pixels in a block are not independent. A class in few blocks gets a wide interval.
- 2026-11-12: v2 results, spatial CV. OA 0.891 [0.868, 0.913], Kappa 0.882 [0.857, 0.904]. Random split OA 0.955, so a random split hides about 59% of the errors (4.5% vs 10.9%). Per-class F1 with CI and blocks: Cotton 0.966 [0.950, 0.978] 45 blocks, Developed 0.964, Barren 0.943, Alfalfa 0.937, Woody 0.937, Shrubland 0.890, Orchards 0.887 [0.722, 0.971] 40 blocks, Small grains 0.867, Other crops 0.865 [0.772, 0.907], Grass/Pasture 0.857, Corn+Sorghum 0.851 [0.806, 0.888], Fallow/Idle 0.833, Double crop 0.781 [0.687, 0.841] 21 blocks.
- 2026-11-12: What the split changed. Other crops F1 rose from 0.814 to 0.865. Corn+Sorghum fell from 0.922 to 0.851 because it is now confused with Double crop in both directions. Double crop is mostly predicted as its own component crops, Corn+Sorghum (18%) and Small grains (9%), which makes sense for an annual embedding of a winter grain followed by corn. Orchards is held up by one place. A single block near Maricopa (lon -111.88, lat 33.07) holds 902 of 4000 Orchards samples and has 43% error, which is why its interval is the widest (0.25). Double crop has 60% of samples in 3 blocks. The largest random vs spatial gaps are now Double crop +0.19, Corn+Sorghum +0.12, Orchards +0.09.

- 2026-11-13: MLflow tracking. Training stays local (52,000 rows, about 30 s per CV run) and logs to the Databricks workspace MLflow. Each run has RF params, CV settings, 62 metrics (pooled, 95% block-bootstrap bounds, per-class F1 with bounds and block counts, per-fold values), the results JSON and confusion figure, and tags for git commit and the SHA-256 of the sample file. The experiment path is built from the signed-in user at run time, so no user name is in the code. No model registered in Unity Catalog yet. Register a final model in Stage 4 when it is needed for maps.

- 2026-11-13: Stage 4 grid. 200 m nodes on CONUS Albers (EPSG:5070, the CDL grid), 348,004 nodes per year, same nodes in 2023 and 2024. Each node is the single 10 m embedding pixel at the node, forced with reproject at 10 m. Without that Earth Engine reads averaged pyramid levels, which are not real embeddings. Vector norms 0.991 to 1.009 confirm real pixels. Pulled with ee.data.computePixels in 64x64 node tiles, 8 in parallel, about 2.5 min per year, no export or Cloud Storage. Class shares match the county CDL shares from Stage 2. Grid labels share one function (`cdl_layers`) with the Stage 2 sampling, checked to give identical labels at 300 stored sample points.
- 2026-11-13: Endpoint choice. Free Edition allows one AI Search endpoint with one search unit and no Direct Access. Standard endpoint, TRIGGERED Delta Sync index with self-managed 64-d vectors. Managed through databricks-sdk, which works with the CLI OAuth login.
- 2026-11-13: Change score (1 - cosine of 2023 and 2024 embeddings) vs CDL transitions, pure nodes in both years. AUC against unchanged nodes: crop to crop 0.98, crop and fallow 0.97, farm and natural 0.86, to or from developed 0.60, natural to natural 0.56. All changes together 0.84. The score detects real farm changes very well. For flips between natural classes (shrub, grass, barren, woody) it is near chance. Together with the Stage 2 observation that Grass/Pasture swings a lot between CDL years, this suggests many of those flips are CDL relabeling rather than land change, though it does not prove it. Among nodes CDL calls unchanged, the top 1% of change scores are mostly Fallow/Idle and Shrubland, worth a look as possible real changes CDL misses.
- 2026-11-13: Tile seam. The embedding images are 163.84 km UTM 12N tiles meeting at easting 500,000 m (111 W). Stable pure shrubland within 5 km of that line has median change 0.027 west and 0.030 east, mean 0.030 vs 0.041. Small next to farm changes (around 0.4) but visible on the change map at low values. Likely a processing tile artifact, not proven.
- 2026-11-13: Search evaluation, 2024, 30 pure query nodes per class, top 50 matches by exact cosine. Share of matches beyond 10 km with the query's class: Shrubland 0.97, Cotton 0.93, Alfalfa 0.80, Small grains 0.79, Corn+Sorghum 0.77, Developed 0.62, Fallow 0.60, Orchards 0.47, Other crops 0.44, Grass/Pasture 0.40, Double crop 0.37, Barren 0.29, Woody 0.28. Counting nearby matches too inflates it most for Barren (0.62), Other crops (0.67) and Woody (0.42). Matches are not restricted to pure nodes, so field-edge labels pull these down a little. Example cotton query: 94% of the top 300 matches beyond 10 km are CDL cotton.

- 2026-11-13: H3 in Databricks SQL. The grid is rolled up to H3 resolution 7 hexagons (2,455 cells, about 142 nodes each) entirely in SQL. The change score is computed in SQL as 1 minus the dot product of the 2023 and 2024 embedding arrays (`zip_with` and `aggregate`), and matches the Python result exactly (mean 0.046772, 16,847 nodes above 0.14 in both). A node counts as changed above 0.14, about the 99th percentile among pure nodes CDL calls unchanged. Across hexagons the embedding change share and the CDL change share correlate at r = 0.67. The maps differ in a telling way. CDL reports change across wide natural areas (shrub and grass flips), the embeddings concentrate on the farm belt.
- 2026-11-13: A burn scar CDL does not show. Of 6 hexagons with over 50% embedding change and under 5% cropland, 5 sit fully inside an area the MODIS burned area product (MCD64A1) maps as burned in 2024 (median burn day 194, mid July), not in 2022 or 2023. Near lon -110.93, lat 32.78. CDL still labels it Shrubland, so its class change share there is only 2 to 19%. This is a real change the embeddings catch and the class labels miss. The same place is the Woody block with 42% error in the Stage 3 failure analysis, which suggests the fire explains part of those errors (not tested). The fire was not identified by name.

- 2026-11-13: Vector Search index ready after about 34 minutes of initial sync (696,008 vectors). Against exact cosine search over 20 random 2024 queries, top 50 overlap averages 0.84 (lowest 0.54, in dense shrubland). Every swapped-in result is within 0.005 cosine of the exact 50th result, and the share of matches in the query's CDL class is the same (0.854 exact, 0.857 index). The approximation does not change answer quality. Query latency from the laptop through the SDK, network included, is median 246 ms and 90th percentile 314 ms. The write-up says "about a quarter of a second", not milliseconds.
- 2026-11-13: Architecture diagram (SVG, renders on GitHub, own light background) and a public write-up in docs/overview.md. Every platform claim was checked in the workspace first. Delta history for samples_pinal has 18 versions. Unity Catalog lineage links volume files to tables and the grid table to the index. A private preview page combines both with the key figures. Nothing pushed to GitHub. The plan is to push once, when the whole project is done.
